## Section 1 — Summary of what was done

ALF-RS-10 is the point in the retail journey where a paid, reserved deal becomes an internal **Sales Order**, and where the vehicle's order status (On Order → In Transit → Arrived) is meant to flow in from Keyloop so Sales can see where the car is and tell the customer.

I grounded the twelve written test cases against what is actually built before running anything: the Sales Order is **not** a separate object — it is a set of fields on the Opportunity (`AF_SOStatus__c`, `AF_SODocumentReference__c`, `AF_SOGeneratedDate__c`, `AF_SOPaymentConfirmedBy__c`) plus a generated document, driven by the `AF_Generate_Sales_Order` Quick Action → screen flow `AF_FL_Opp_GenerateSalesOrder` → Apex `AF_SalesOrderService`, gated by validation rule `AF_VR_Opp_SOGenerationGate`. Vehicle order status lives in `AF_VehicleOrderStatusSync` writing `Vehicle.AF_VehicleOrderState__c`. All of that was read from the retrieved metadata, not from descriptions.

I then built the scenario set (13 Opportunities, 9 Vehicles, 8 Reservations, 8 Invoice Summaries and their payments, plus a 150-record fleet account) and executed every test case API-first as the Sales Representative, then through the real Lightning click-path as the same persona, re-testing every failure as System Administrator to separate build defects from permission gaps.

**Headline verdict: ALF-RS-10 does not work for anyone today.** A Sales Order cannot be generated by any user, including System Administrator, because the gate requires a field (`Opportunity.AF_VehicleReservation__c`) that no automation in the org ever fills in. Even with that field populated by hand, generation still fails for every business persona because the Apex writes an audit field none of them may edit. Behind those two blockers the design is largely sound — the gate logic, the payment-confirmation prompt, the duplicate guard, the out-of-order sync protection and the bulk behaviour all do the right thing — but FR2 (the Keyloop synchronisation) is a stub with no integration behind it, FR3's order states are never shown to a user anywhere in the UI, and the Sales Representative can read every business unit's deals.

## Section 3 — BA / requirement gaps to raise

These are not defects. The build cannot be called wrong because the documents never said what right was — each needs a business decision, not a developer.

### GAP-RS10-01 — FR4 says full payment; the Solution Design says first payment. Both are still live documents.

**The situation:** the build enforces **full payment** before Sales Order generation. The Solution Design says the opposite.

**What the documents say:** BRD p. 52, §10.10 FR4 — *"The system should prompt the Sales Representative to confirm full payment collection before allowing the generation of the Sales Order document."* Solution Design p. 47, DEC-014 *Sales Order timing* — *"the Sales Order document is produced after the first payment/deposit; full payment is required before handover, not before the Sales Order."*

The build chose FR4 (confirmed in `AF_VR_Opp_SOGenerationGate`'s own description: *"supersedes DEC-014 first-payment-only, 2026-09-19"*), but DEC-014 is still in the SD and its wording is still printed on the customer-facing contract (BUG-RS10-07). **Decision needed from the BA:** withdraw or amend DEC-014 so one rule stands, and confirm which commercial moment the Sales Order represents.

### GAP-RS10-02 — A customer can pay in full for a car that can then never receive a Sales Order.

**The situation:** ALF-RS-08 lets the business reserve and take money for in-transit and pre-ordered vehicles. ALF-RS-10 then refuses to produce the Sales Order for exactly those vehicles. Reproduced with real money on a real record: DEMO RS10 11 took 500,000 in full and is now stuck.

**What the documents say:** BRD p. 52, §10.10 FR5 — *"The system should prevent Sales Order generation for opportunities linked to non-stock, on-order, or in-transit vehicles, consistent with the Keyloop Sales Order logic."* BRD §10.8 ALF-RS-08 FR4 — reservations are supported for in-stock and in-transit or ordered vehicles, with placeholder representation for pre-orders.

Neither document defines what the customer's position is between payment and arrival, or what document they hold in the meantime. **Decision needed from the BA:** either the Sales Order is allowed for in-transit/pre-order vehicles, or a distinct interim document/state is defined, or deposits are restricted on those vehicles.

### GAP-RS10-03 — The brands the story is written around are not in the build.

BRD p. 53, §10.10 Assumption 1 — *"For BMW and JLR special orders where the required vehicle is not available in stock, the vehicle ordering and planning team must complete their internal checks and approval before the Sales team can proceed to downpayment collection."* The org offers Ferrari, Rolls-Royce and Undecided only, and nothing maps to the *Alfardan Premier Motors* business unit. **Decision needed:** confirm the Release 1 brand scope. If BMW/JLR are in scope, brand values, business-unit mapping and the Premier Motors sharing path are all missing; if they are not, Assumption 1 and the BMW/JLR test scenarios should be deferred with the rest of that scope.

### GAP-RS10-04 — The Ordering / Planning / Admin Team has no definition and no representation.

ALF-RS-10 names it in Related Personas, but BRD §6 System Personas does not define it, and it exists nowhere in the org: no permission set group, no role, no profile. **Decision needed:** define the role, its responsibilities and its data rights (TC-011 expects it to update order-tracking fields but not commercial fields such as agreed price — that segregation is not written down anywhere).

### GAP-RS10-05 — FR3 and the Requirement Overview name the same state differently, and the build now has two fields.

BRD p. 52, §10.10 FR3 — *"high-level order states of **On Order**, In Transit, and In Stock / Arrived"*; the Requirement Overview on the same page — *"Vehicle order statuses (**Ordered**, In Transit, Arrived) are synchronized from Keyloop into Salesforce."* The build has both: `AF_VehicleOrderState__c` (On Order / In Transit / In Stock-Arrived) and `AF_InventoryStatus__c` (In Stock / In Transit / **Ordered**). **Decision needed:** one canonical vocabulary, and an explicit statement of the Keyloop value → Salesforce value mapping.

### GAP-RS10-06 — AC3 describes a sequence the rules make impossible.

BRD p. 53, §10.10 AC3 — *"Given a vehicle arrives In Stock, When the status syncs, Then the customer receives a progress notification and the sales order is updated."* But FR5 only allows a Sales Order once the vehicle is already In Stock, and the notification only fires when a Sales Order already exists. The two conditions cannot both hold in sequence. **Decision needed:** confirm whether the arrival notification should fire for deals with a reservation but no Sales Order yet (which would also resolve GAP-RS10-02's customer-communication problem).

### GAP-RS10-07 — "Meaningful order progress updates at appropriate stages" is never defined.

BRD p. 52, §10.10 FR6 — *"The system should notify customers and internal users of meaningful order progress updates at appropriate stages."* Only arrival is implemented; the In Transit transition notifies nobody. **Decision needed:** the list of notifiable stages, the recipients for each, and whether a transactional exemption applies to customers who have opted out of marketing communications (the test cases assume one; nothing states it).

### GAP-RS10-08 — Nothing says whether the Sales Order is customer-facing.

The Requirement Overview calls it *"an internal document… stored against the opportunity for internal processing"*, yet the generated file is a *"NEW VEHICLE SALE CONTRACT"* with buyer acknowledgements and a signature block for both parties. **Decision needed:** is this document signed by the customer, and if so, by what route (it is currently never sent to anyone)?

### GAP-RS10-09 — No alerting requirement exists for integration failures.

TC-005 and TC-009 expect an alert when the sync fails or receives an unmapped value; BRD §7 Cross-Cutting 3 requires integration monitoring in general terms but names no recipient, channel or threshold for this story. **Decision needed:** who is alerted, how, and how quickly, when a vehicle status message fails.

## Section 5 — Hands-on: run ALF-RS-10 yourself

### What this story is, and how it was built

In business terms: the customer has agreed, the car is reserved, the money is in. The Sales Representative now produces the **Sales Order** — Alfardan's own internal sale contract. It never leaves the building: it is not sent to BMW or Ferrari, and it has nothing to do with how the ordering team actually orders cars in Autoline. From that point the story's second job is visibility: as the car moves *On Order → In Transit → Arrived* in Keyloop, those statuses should appear in Salesforce so Sales can see where it is and tell the customer when it lands.

How it is actually built:

- **There is no Sales Order object.** The "Sales Order" is four fields on the Opportunity — *Sales Order Status*, *Sales Order Document Reference*, *Sales Order Generated Date*, *Sales Order Payment Confirmed By* — plus a generated file under Notes & Attachments. You will find them in the **Sales Order** section of the Opportunity's Details tab.
- **The button** is *Generate Sales Order* under **Show more actions**. It runs a screen flow that asks you to confirm payment, then calls Apex (`AF_SalesOrderService`) which builds the contract, attaches it, sets the status and flips the reservation to *Converted*.
- **The gate** exists twice, deliberately: as a validation rule (so no save path anywhere can dodge it) and inside the Apex (so you get a readable message instead of a raw error). It requires: invoice **Fully Paid**, a **selected vehicle that is In Stock**, and a **Reserved reservation on the Opportunity for that same vehicle**.
- **Vehicle status** has two separate fields that do not talk to each other: *Inventory Status* (what you can see) and *Vehicle Order State* (what the Keyloop sync writes — and which is on no page at all).
