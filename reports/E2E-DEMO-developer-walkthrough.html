Before the demo: fixes that must be deployed

Two bugs stop the demo on screen and must be fixed and deployed to QA first. Two more can be avoided by following this run-book, and one feature is left out of the demo.

Do a full rehearsal first

Run every deal once, end to end, the day before, using a spare set of records. The records named in this run-book are used up by the live run. Ask QA to create a fresh set for the rehearsal, or afterwards if you have already rehearsed on these.

Logging in

  • Passwords are shared by QA separately.
  • Login is two steps: username, then password.
  • If Change Your Password appears, click Cancel.
  • Switch apps with the App Launcher (9 dots) → Automotive.

Approving things

  • Approvals appear on the approver's Home page under Items to Approve.
  • Clicking the record name does not open the approval. Use the row's ▾ Show Actions → Approve instead.
  • Refresh Home if a new item hasn't appeared yet.

Files to upload

Sample PDFs and images are in demo-docs/e2e/ in the QA repo. Copy them to the demo laptop's Desktop. Wait for each upload to show Done before you leave the page, or it won't be recorded.

Deal 1 · main storyline · about 35 minutes

Cash sale with discount, from showroom walk-in to delivery

CustomerNasser Al-Attiyah
Car296 GTB · Rosso Imola
DepositAED 45,000 (above 10%)
PaymentBank transfer

  1. Create the deal with Sales Walk-In

    Receptionist
    Contacts → Nasser Al-Attiyah → ▾ Show more actions → Sales Walk-In
    1. Brand Ferrari, Showroom Lusail, Source of Business Showroom Walk-in → Next.
    2. Sales Representative QA SalesRep2 → Next.

    Expect: "The Opportunity has been created under this Account and assigned to the selected Sales Representative." The rep gets a bell notification and a task.

    If "Something went wrong" appears (LFRDN-752 not yet deployed): the deal was created. Don't run it again. Go on to window 2.

  2. Open the new deal

    Sales Rep
    Bell icon → the walk-in notification, or Opportunities → Recently Viewed / My Opportunities

    Expect: the deal at stage Consider, owned by QA SalesRep2.

  3. Select the vehicle

    Sales Rep
    Opportunity → Select Vehicle
    1. Pick model Ferrari 296 GTB.
    2. Pick the car STK-DEMO-E2E-01 (Rosso Imola).
    3. Pick a line package if offered (e.g. GTS Package) → Confirm Line.

    Expect: the car appears on the deal and the amount is filled in (≈ AED 398,300 with the GTS package).

  4. Move to Explore

    Sales Rep
    Path at the top → Mark Stage as Complete

    Expect: stage Explore. A Test Drive and an empty Trade In are created automatically under Related. The customer is emailed a "trade in your car" invitation.

  5. Book the test drive

    Sales Rep
    Opportunity → Related → Test Drives → open the TD record → Select Demo Vehicle
    1. Showroom Lusail → pick a demo car and a free slot → Confirm Booking.

    Expect: Test Drive status Scheduled.

  6. Complete the test drive

    Sales Rep
    Test Drive → Edit
    1. Status Completed, Result Completed, Product Feedback Loved the handling; wants the GTS package → Save.

    Expect: Test Drive Completed.

  7. Move to Select and add an accessory

    Sales Rep
    Opportunity → Path → Mark Stage as Complete → then Add Products
    1. Add a window tint (or any accessory) → Save.

    Expect: stage Select. The accessory is added to the products.

  8. Create the quotation

    Sales Rep
    Opportunity → ▾ Show more actions → Create Quotation
    1. Choose Customer Quotation → finish.

    Expect: a Quote under Related → Quotes. Its lines show the specific car, the package and the accessory. Note the quote's Subtotal (e.g. AED 399,500).

  9. Request a discount

    Sales Rep
    Quote → Edit → Discount Amount
    1. Enter exactly 3% of the Subtotal. For a Subtotal of 399,500, that's 11,985. Save.

    Expect: Discount % 3.00, Discount Tier 1, status Pending Approval. A discount of 2% or less needs no approval.

    Pick an amount that makes a round percentage. The percentage is stored with two decimals and applied to each line, so a figure like 10,000 (2.503%) comes out a few dirhams short.

  10. Approve the discount

    Sales/Brand Manager
    Home → Items to Approve → row ▾ → Approve

    Expect: Quote discount status Approved. The Grand Total drops by the amount you entered.

  11. Sync the quote

    Sales Rep
    Quote → ▾ Show more actions → Start Sync → Sync

    Expect: the quote is marked as syncing. Without this, the move to Commit later is refused with "Commit stage requires a synced Quote."

  12. Create the reservation, 7-Day with deposit

    Sales Rep
    Quote → Create Reservation
    1. Reservation type 7-Day. Leave Trade-In Used As Deposit? unticked.
    2. Deposit Amount 45,000 (more than 10% of the price) → finish.

    Expect: a Reservation (RES-…) with status Reserved straight away, and approval Not Required. An invoice is created on the deal.

    If there is no deposit field and "We couldn't create the reservation" appears, LFRDN-695 is not deployed. Stop the live flow and switch to a deal QA pre-reserved.

  13. Take the payment

    Sales Rep
    Opportunity → Invoice Summary → open the invoice → Get Payment
    1. Payment reference KL-PAY-DEMO-01 → Next → No, I'm done.

    Expect: Payment Paid / Verified (Bank Transfer); invoice Fully Paid. Get Payment always records the full balance, not only the deposit.

  14. Upload the Bank LPO, straight away

    Sales Rep
    Payment (PAY-…) → Related → Required Documents → Bank LPO → Upload

    Expect: the Bank LPO row reads Uploaded.

    Don't skip this. Without the LPO, the deal silently stays open after the handover (LFRDN-754).

  15. Reservation documents and contract

    Sales Rep
    Reservation → Related → Required Documents → then the header button Generate Reservation Contract
    1. Upload a file against each listed document (4 for this deal). Wait for Done each time.
    2. Click Generate Reservation Contract.

    Expect: every document row reads Uploaded. The contract PDF appears under Notes & Attachments / Files.

  16. Move to Commit

    Sales Rep
    Opportunity → Path → Mark Stage as Complete

    Expect: stage Commit.

  17. Generate the Sales Order

    Sales Rep
    Opportunity → ▾ Show more actions → Generate Sales Order
    1. Choose Yes, full payment has been received → Next.

    Expect: the Sales Order is generated and its PDF attached.

    The confirmation and the PDF say QAR instead of AED (LFRDN-678, deferred). Don't dwell on the PDF.

  18. Move to Take the Keys

    Sales Rep
    Opportunity → Path → Mark Stage as Complete

    Expect: stage Take the Keys. A Handover (HO-…) is created with the car, contact, account and invoice linked, plus 5 agreements generated automatically.

  19. Schedule the handover

    Sales Rep
    Handover → Edit
    1. Handover Location Lusail, Status In Progress, Date = tomorrow.
    2. Time: clear the pre-filled "12:00 PM" first, then type e.g. 4:00 PM.
    3. Repair Remarks, e.g. Minor stone chip on front bumper touched up → Save.

    Enter the remarks before Generate Documents. Adding them afterwards duplicates the repair disclaimer (LFRDN-708).

  20. Generate the delivery documents

    Sales Rep
    Handover header → Generate Documents

    Expect: the Delivery Note is generated.

  21. Upload Traffic and Insurance forms

    Sales Rep
    Handover → Related → required documents → Traffic Form and Insurance Form → Upload

    Expect: both rows read Uploaded.

  22. Customer signs off, and the deal closes

    Sales Rep System
    Handover → Edit → Customer Sign-Off Physically Signed → Save

    Expect: Handover Completed, Sales Order Delivered, and on refreshing the Opportunity, stage Closed Won automatically.

    If the stage is still Take the Keys: check the Bank LPO on the Payment, then edit and re-save the handover (LFRDN-754).

Deal 2 · trade-in · about 25 minutes

Trade-in valuation and a manager-approved low deposit

CustomerAisha Al-Marri
Car296 GTB · Giallo Modena
Trade-inRange Rover Sport · AED 135,000
DepositAED 10,000 (below 10%)
No discount on this deal

A discount on a quote with a trade-in comes off at the wrong amount (LFRDN-755). The discount is shown in Deal 1 only.

  1. Walk-in, vehicle, Explore, test drive

    Receptionist Sales Rep
    Repeat Deal 1 steps 1–6 with Contact Aisha Al-Marri and car STK-DEMO-E2E-02 (Giallo Modena)

    Expect: deal at Explore, test drive Completed, an empty Trade In (APL-…) under Related.

  2. Add the customer's car to the trade-in

    Sales Rep
    Opportunity → Related → Trade Ins → open APL-… → Related → Trade In Items card → New
    • Type Vehicle; VIN SALWA2FK7MA123456; Make Land Rover; Model Range Rover Sport; Year 2021; Plate 123456
    • Condition and Usage (any values, e.g. mileage 42,000); Inspection Done ✓ with notes Full service history
    • Source, and Asking Value 150,000 → Save

    Use the New button inside the Trade In Items card. The New at the top of the page opens a different record type (Appraisal Adjustment).

  3. Attach the 360° photo and submit

    Sales Rep
    Trade In Item → Related → Files → Upload an image → back to the Trade In → Submit for Evaluation

    Expect: "Submitted". Submitting without an item and a photo is refused, which is worth showing once.

  4. Value the car

    Used Car Team
    Trade In Item (APLI-…) → Edit → Initial Value 135,000 → Save

    Expect: the valuation goes for approval to the Used Car Team queue.

  5. Approve the valuation

    Used Car Team
    Home → Items to Approve → row ▾ → Approve

    Expect: the trade-in header shows Trade-In Outcome Accepted.

  6. Record the customer's acceptance

    Sales Rep
    Trade In Item → Edit → Trade-In Outcome Accepted → Save

    Expect: the Trade In's Active Trade-In Value reads 135,000.

    Do this before creating the quote. Until the vehicle line itself is Accepted, the quote has no trade-in deduction (LFRDN-756).

  7. Select, then Create Quotation

    Sales Rep
    Path → Mark Stage as Complete (Select) → ▾ Show more actions → Create Quotation → Customer Quotation

    Expect: the quote includes a −135,000 Trade-In Deduction line, and the Grand Total is net of the trade-in.

  8. Sync the quote

    Sales Rep
    Quote → ▾ Show more actions → Start Sync → Sync

  9. Create the reservation with a low deposit

    Sales Rep
    Quote → Create Reservation
    1. Type 7-Day; Trade-In Used As Deposit? unticked; Deposit Amount 10,000 → finish.

    Expect: Reservation status Pending Approval, because the deposit is under the Ferrari 10% threshold.

  10. Manager approves the low deposit

    Sales/Brand Manager
    Home → Items to Approve → row ▾ → Approve

    Expect: Reservation Reserved. Its Required Documents now list 5 items, including an Approval Reference.

  11. Finish the deal

    Sales Rep
    Continue with Deal 1 steps 13–22 (payment, Bank LPO, reservation documents, Commit, Sales Order, handover)

    Expect: Closed Won. The invoice amount is the net price after the trade-in.

    Short on time? Stop after the approved reservation and say the rest is the same as Deal 1.

Service Walk-In: explain it, don't click it

Service Walk-In is for a customer at service reception who says they are interested in a new car. The Service Receptionist runs it on the Contact and picks the brand and preferred dealership. The deal goes to that dealership's Showroom Manager with a "contact within 15 minutes" task, and the manager then hands it to a rep. From there the cycle is the same as Deal 1.

Leave it out of the live run unless all three are true
  • LFRDN-753 is fixed and deployed. Today the flow fails with "An unhandled fault has occurred" and creates nothing.
  • A Service Receptionist user exists. There is none in QA today.
  • The chosen dealership has a Showroom Manager. Only Alsadd has one today.

If all three are ready: Service Receptionist → Contact Latifa Al-Kuwari → ▾ Show more actions → Service Walk-In → Brand of Interest Ferrari, Preferred Dealership Alsadd, Source Showroom Walk-in → Next. Then, as the Showroom Manager, open the deal → Change Owner → QA SalesRep2. The rep gets an "Opportunity Assigned to You" task.
