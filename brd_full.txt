  2.Document Purpose

This Business Requirements Document (BRD) serves as the foundation for the detailed
solution design of the Salesforce Automotive Cloud implementation for Alfardan Group
Release 1 scope. The content outlined herein will directly inform and guide the
technical design, configuration, development, and integration activities, ensuring
alignment between stakeholder expectations and the delivered solution.

Arcsen Technologies | Confidential  4               © 2026 Arcsen. All Rights Reserved
It aims to:
1. Clearly articulate the business problem or opportunity.
2. Define the functional requirements from a business and system perspective.
3. Provide a baseline for solution design, development, and implementation.
4. Support stakeholder consensus and serve as a point of reference throughout the

    project lifecycle.
This BRD is intended to be reviewed and approved by business stakeholders, project
sponsors, and the technical implementation team to ensure mutual understanding and
alignment before proceeding to subsequent phases of the project.

  4. Scope

1. Business Units in current release: Alfardan Automobiles, Alfardan Premier Motors,
    and Alfardan Sports Motors.

    Retail Sales / Opportunity Management.

3. Platform scope: Salesforce Automotive Cloud as the core business platform, with

  5.Business Objectives

1. Standardize lead, case, service appointment, and retail sales processes across the
    in-scope automotive business units while preserving brand-specific operational
    needs.

2. Implement Salesforce Automotive Cloud as the primary customer engagement and
    process orchestration layer for CRM, service, and retail sales journeys.

    structured routing, SLAs, approvals, and dashboards.

4. Enable controlled integration with Keyloop Autoline/Drive via MuleSoft while
    minimizing manual re-entry and improving data consistency.

5. Provide a scalable Release 1 foundation that can later be extended to other
    Alfardan business units and future capabilities.

  7.Cross-Cutting Requirements

1. Customer profile visibility is shared at account level, while opportunities, cases,

   service appointments, quotations, and other transactional records remain
   segregated by business unit, brand and department as per the security model.

2. Keyloop Autoline/Drive remains the source of truth for major DMS-controlled data

   areas such as inventory, workshop capacity, and selected service / financial

Arcsen Technologies | Confidential  10  © 2026 Arcsen. All Rights Reserved
   records, with Salesforce acting as the customer engagement and orchestration
   layer.

3. MuleSoft will serve as the middleware layer for Salesforce-to-Keyloop and all related

   integration touchpoints.

4. Salesforce Marketing Cloud will be leveraged for outbound customer

   communications, nurture journeys, and campaign audience management.

5. Salesforce Data Cloud will serve as the unified data platform supporting customer

   identity resolution and cross-channel analytics.

6. Where a process differs by brand or business unit, this BRD defines the unified

   baseline process and notes brand-specific deviations where they materially affect
   design.

7. Approvals, reminders, and escalations should be configurable and fully traceable

   through Salesforce.

8. Integration touchpoints with Keyloop Autoline/Drive systems are referenced within

   the relevant process sections and will be detailed further in the integration register.

9. Reports and dashboards across all in-scope processes will be owned and

   maintained primarily by the CRM Manager and CRM Supervisor. CRM Agents will
   have view-only access to dashboards relevant to their assigned work. Data export
   permissions on reports and dashboards will be restricted to CRM Manager, CRM
   Supervisor, and the System Administrator via Salesforce profile configuration; CRM
   Agent profiles will not include export access by default. SLA timers across all in-
   scope processes (Lead Management, Case Management, and Service
   Appointments) will respect the configured working calendar in Salesforce.

10. Records received outside working hours will have their SLA clock activated from

   the start of the next working period. Working hours, business days, and any off-day
   exclusions (including Fridays) will be configured and maintained by the System
   Administrator as part of the initial platform setup.

11. Senior managers across CRM, Sales, and Aftersales should be able to

   independently create, modify, and link reports and dashboards within their permitted
   data scope. Export to Excel must be enabled for senior management profiles.

12. Reports and dashboards requirements to be confirmed during the solution

   design phase.

13. All reports and dashboards will be scoped to the user's own business unit by

   default, in line with the agreed security model. Cross-BU reporting access, if
   required, to be defined and approved during the design phase.

14. Cisco will serve as the Computer Telephony Integration (CTI) layer between the

   Alfardan business-unit telephony systems and Salesforce, preserving the existing
   per-business-unit telephony separation while bringing inbound and outbound calls
   into the unified Salesforce customer journey. The integration is intended to support:
   (a) Lead Management, where inbound calls received by CRM agents are converted
   into manual Lead records with the Lead Source field defaulted to "Incoming Call",
   supporting the agreed manual lead capture flow; (b) Case Management, where
   Cisco CTI provides screen-pop with verified customer identification on inbound calls

Arcsen Technologies | Confidential  11  © 2026 Arcsen. All Rights Reserved
so CRM agents can greet, authenticate, and contextualise the customer in real time,
with optional case creation directly from the call interaction for sales, after-sales,
and general inquiries; (c) Service Appointments, where Service Reception and CRM
users can capture, modify, and confirm a Service Appointment booking on the
customer's behalf during a live inbound call, mirroring the chatbot and web booking
journeys; and (d) Customer 360 visibility, where every inbound and outbound call is
automatically logged as an activity record on the relevant Account, Lead, Case, or
Service Appointment for full interaction history. Cisco-specific configuration, per-
business-unit number routing, and call-recording requirements will be detailed
further in the integration register and the relevant process sections.

8.3 Lead Duplicate Detection at Intake

User Story Title      [ALF-LM-03] Lead Duplicate Detection at Intake
Related Personas
User Story            System, CRM Executive / Agent
Requirement Overview
                      As a system, I should check lead duplicates at intake so
Functional            that the team can reduce duplicate customer records
Requirements          without blocking valid business activity.
                      The system performs duplicate checks on incoming leads

                      flexibility while improving data quality.
                      1. The system should perform a duplicate check on every

                          customer records using phone number and email

                          duplicate blocking rule for digital / automated leads to
                          prevent rapid re-submission from the same contact.
                      4. The system should display the matched existing
                          record(s) to the agent so they can make an informed
                          decision to either proceed or tackle the existing reco.

Arcsen Technologies | Confidential  16  © 2026 Arcsen. All Rights Reserved
Acceptance Criteria   1. Given a new lead is created with a phone or email
Assumptions/Comments      matching an existing record, When the lead is saved,
                          Then the system displays a duplicate warning and
                          surfaces the matched record.

                      2. Given a duplicate warning is shown, When the CRM
                          agent reviews and decides to proceed, Then the system
                          allows the lead to be saved without blocking.

                      3. Given the configurable short-window blocking rule is
